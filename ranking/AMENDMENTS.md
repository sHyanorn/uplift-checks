## 2026-11-22: before any search

No search trial, final fit or test read had run and no result existed. Each entry quotes the registered sentence and states what the code does. None changes a statistic, a decision rule or a label.

1. Section 11, "EconML `n_jobs=1` with `random_state` (separate configurations run as separate processes)". The forest configurations of a unit run one after another in one process. With `n_jobs=1` and a given `random_state`, a fit gave the same predictions in a process of its own and after other configurations in the same process (three configurations on synthetic data, in both orders), so the processes are not separated.
2. Section 5.2, "V1 rescales V4". This is exact when the ranking starts with a control row, or when no row up to and including the first control row is a responder. Otherwise causalml 0.18.0 gets 0 / 0 before the first control row and joins its curve linearly over those rows, where V4 has `Q(k) = Y_T(k)`. V1 stays causalml's `qini_score` as called, and no rule reads the sentence.
3. Section 5.4, "fitted in two folds on training rows, clipped to [0.01, 0.99]; on validation rows `e` is the mean of the two fold models". On validation rows the predictions of the two fold models are averaged and the mean is clipped. A training row's `e` is the clipped prediction of the fold model that did not see it. The same holds for `e` within a learner (Section 6).
4. Section 5.2, V5 "used for the units of L4 only". V5 is computed on every unit, since H4 asks for the MDD for "each unit and own version (including V2* and V5)"; it enters a label only through L4, on U1. Where `q(1) = 0`, V5 is 0 (Section 5.1: a ratio with a zero denominator is taken as 0).
5. Section 10, "it then writes an entry (UTC time, commit, manifest hash), which is committed before any test row is read". Between its checks and that entry, the entry point now rehearses the read. The frozen models predict synthetic rows, as many as the dataset has test rows (the count only: no test row is opened); each feature is drawn on its own from the validation rows' values and (W, Y) as pairs from the validation rows, with `numpy.random.default_rng([20261115, 7, unit])`. Every output of Sections 7 and 8 is then computed on them with 16 replicates. If the peak memory of the process and its workers is above 12 GiB, 3 GiB under the container of Section 11, the entry point stops before it leaves a token or writes an entry. The stop is handled as Section 10 handles "a fit that exceeds the container's memory": reported and resolved by an amendment before the read. Otherwise the entry also holds that peak. Why: runs on synthetic rows of Criteo's shape at up to 2,000,000 rows, extrapolated to Criteo's test size, put the read as first written above 15 GiB, after its token was gone.
6. Section 10, "a trial record whose fingerprint of `m̂` and `e` differs from that of the unit's first record (Section 11)". The records compared are those in use. Section 11: "Records written before a change to a model's fitting code are not used"; such a record neither sets the reference nor stops a run. The reference is the unit's first record in use. The search stops if its own refit or a record in use differs from it, and `block` stops on such a record before it selects.

Section 7, H2, "the median `MDD_sim` of this family and the median marginal MDD over the 36 pairs under V6 and under V11", and Section 8, H5, "the median over models of the ratio of AIPW to IPW interval widths". Each sentence allows two readings and both are reported: the median `MDD_sim` over the 72 differences and under each metric; the median ratio over all models and budgets and at each budget. No rule reads either.
8. Section 8, H5, "the median over models of the ratio of AIPW to IPW interval widths". A (model, budget) pair whose IPW interval has width 0 is left out of the median under both readings of entry 7, and a median with no pair left is reported as null. The width is 0 when the contrast with R* is 0 in every replicate (Section 5.5). A model whose scores rank the rows as R*'s do is such a case: its AIPW width is 0 too and the ratio is 0 / 0. Section 5.1's "A ratio with a zero denominator is taken as 0" is not applied to this ratio: a 0 would enter the median as a width ratio that no pair has. The pair stays in the H5 output with both intervals and labels. No rule reads the ratio.
